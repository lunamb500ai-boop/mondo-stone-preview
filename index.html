What we do

A stonemason, not a showroom.

Mondo Stone is a stonemasonry business. Our work is the making and fitting: the site measure, the template, the cutting and polishing, and the install.

That is where a stone job is won or lost. A mitre that closes cleanly, a join placed where the veining hides it, a cut-out that suits the sink you actually bought. If you already have a slab, we’ll work with it. If you don’t, we’ll help you choose one and supply it.

How a job runs

Measure, template, fabricate, install

  1. Measure

    We visit the site, check cabinets, walls and levels, and talk through edges, joins and cut-outs.

  2. Template

    We template the job so every piece is cut to the room as it is, not as the plan says it should be.

  3. Fabricate

    The stone is cut, mitred, polished and dry-checked, with veining laid out so it flows across joins.

  4. Install

    We deliver, set and join the stone on site, seal it where the material needs it and leave it clean.
