About

The making and the fitting

Mondo Stone is a stonemasonry business based in Albert Park, working in homes across Melbourne and surrounds.

What you get from us

We are a trade, not a showroom. Our part of the job is the measuring, the cutting and the fitting, and that is what we want to be judged on.

Most of our work is kitchens and bathrooms: benchtops and islands, vanities, splashbacks, slab walls, shower niches and tiling. We also repair and re-polish stone that is already in place.

We can supply the stone, the substrate under it and the installation, so there is one provider to deal with from the first measure to the last join.

How we work

A few things we hold to

  1. 01

    Template the room as built

    Walls are rarely square and cabinets are rarely where the drawing says. We cut to the template, not the plan.

  2. 02

    Plan the joins

    We agree where joins and mitres go before any cutting, and set out the veining so it carries across them.

  3. 03

    Say what the stone will do

    Every material has limits. We will tell you how a stone wears, marks and needs to be looked after before you commit to it.

  4. 04

    Leave it finished

    Stone set, joined, sealed where it needs it and cleaned down, ready for the next trade or for you.
