Services

What we make and fit

Every job runs the same way: we measure, template, fabricate and install. Here is what that covers.

01

Kitchen benchtops & waterfall islands

The benchtop takes more wear than any other surface in the house, so we start with how you cook and what the stone has to put up with. Then we template the cabinets as built and cut every piece to suit.

  • Benchtops, island tops and breakfast bars
  • Waterfall ends and island panels with mitred corners
  • Undermount and flush sink cut-outs, cooktop cut-outs, tap holes
  • Upstands and matching splashbacks
02

Bathroom vanities

A vanity is a small piece that gets looked at closely. We make simple tops and fully mitred vanity boxes that read as one block of stone.

  • Vanity tops for wall-hung and floor-standing cabinets
  • Floating, mitred vanity boxes
  • Undermount and integrated basins
  • Upstands, shelves and ledges in the same stone
03

Full-height slab walls, showers & niches

Slab walls give you stone without grout lines. We plan where panels meet, set out the veining, and cut around tapware, niches and mirrors on site.

  • Feature walls and full-height kitchen walls
  • Shower walls and bath surrounds
  • Niches lined and mitred in the same material
  • Stone and porcelain panels
04

Splashbacks

A splashback cut from the same slab as the bench ties a kitchen together. We set it out so the pattern carries from one surface to the next.

  • Low upstands through to full-height splashbacks
  • Cut-outs for power points, rangehoods and window reveals
  • Mirror or glass splashbacks on request, where they suit the room
05

Floors, walls & bathroom tiling

We also lay stone and porcelain tile, which means the floor, the walls and the vanity in a bathroom renovation can be handled by the same trade.

  • Floor and wall tiling in stone and porcelain
  • Large-format tile
  • Shower floors, hobs and nib walls
  • Bathroom renovations
06

Repairs & re-polishing

Stone that is chipped, dull or opening at a join can often be brought back rather than replaced. We will look at it and tell you honestly what is achievable.

  • Join repairs
  • Chip repairs, from small to major
  • Re-polishing worn or etched surfaces
07

Substrates & 40 mm+ profiles

Stone tops with an edge profile thicker than 20 mm need a substrate under them. That is usually a separate job for a cabinet maker. We supply and fit the substrate ourselves, so upgrading an old bench to a thick stone profile is one booking with one provider.

  • Substrates supplied and fitted
  • Built-up and mitred edges of 40 mm and more
  • Replacing old benchtops on existing cabinets
08

Stone supply

We are happy to fabricate stone you have chosen elsewhere. If you would rather not shop for a slab, we can source and supply it as part of the job.

  • Natural marble, quartzite, granite, onyx and travertine
  • Terrazzo and porcelain slabs
  • Advice on which materials suit kitchens, bathrooms and wet areas
How a job runs

Measure, template, fabricate, install

  1. Measure

    We visit the site, check cabinets, walls and levels, and talk through edges, joins and cut-outs.

  2. Template

    We template the job so every piece is cut to the room as it is, not as the plan says it should be.

  3. Fabricate

    The stone is cut, mitred, polished and dry-checked, with veining laid out so it flows across joins.

  4. Install

    We deliver, set and join the stone on site, seal it where the material needs it and leave it clean.
