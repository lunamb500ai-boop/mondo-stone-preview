Stone

Materials we work with

We fabricate and install natural stone and porcelain. Bring your own slab, or ask us to source and supply one for the job.

Marble

Soft, cool and finely veined. The classic choice for vanities, splashbacks and feature walls, and a good benchtop if you are comfortable with it ageing.

Marble marks with acids such as lemon juice and wine, and needs sealing. It can be re-polished.

Quartzite

A hard natural stone with the movement of marble. A strong choice for kitchen benchtops and islands.

Hardness and porosity vary from slab to slab, so we check each one. Most are sealed.

Granite

Dense, hard-wearing and heat tolerant. Well suited to busy kitchens and outdoor benches.

Usually sealed. Among the lowest-maintenance natural stones.

Onyx

Banded and translucent, and can be backlit. Best for vanities, bars and feature panels.

Soft and sensitive to acids. We don’t recommend it for hard-working kitchen benches.

Travertine

Warm, matte and textured. Used for vanities, walls, floors and tiling.

Naturally pitted; supplied filled or unfilled. Needs sealing.

Terrazzo

Stone chips set in a cement base, then ground and polished. Suits vanities, wall panels, benches and floors.

Chip size and colour vary by batch. Cement-based terrazzo is sealed.

Porcelain & sintered slabs

Thin, large-format slabs fired at high temperature. Used for benchtops, splashbacks, shower walls and cladding.

Very low porosity and no sealing. Edges are mitred, because the pattern is usually on the surface only.

Choosing a slab

Every natural slab is different, so the best way to choose is to see the actual piece.

Tell us where the stone is going and how it will be used. We’ll suggest materials that suit, source slabs for you to look at, and explain what each one needs in the way of sealing and care. We’ll also be straight with you if a stone you like is the wrong fit for the job.

What we work in

Natural marble, quartzite, granite, onyx and travertine, cement-based terrazzo, and porcelain or sintered slabs.
